Hi! I am **Zhicheng (Charlie) Zhang**, a Specially Appointed Researcher at [The University of Osaka](https://www.osaka-u.ac.jp/en), working with [Prof. Sho Shirasaka](https://www.ist.osaka-u.ac.jp/english/researcher/detail.php?id=17) in the [Nonlinear Mathematical Science Lab](http://www-nomo.ist.osaka-u.ac.jp/en_index.html).

From 2024 to 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [JST Moonshot Goal 8 Program](https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/english/moonshot/sub8_en.html) at Kyoto University, mentored by [Prof. Yoshihiko Susuki](https://y-susuki.github.io/index.html). I received my Ph.D. in Informatics from Osaka University in 2024, advised by [Prof. Yasumasa Fujisaki](http://www-door.ist.osaka-u.ac.jp/fujisaki/index.htm); my M.Sc. in Mathematics from Guilin University of Electronic Technology in 2020 (advisor: [Prof. Zhongjun Ma](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/author/37593362000)); and my B.A. in Japanese from Changzhou Institute of Technology in 2017. From 2019 to 2020, I was a visiting master's student at The University of Kitakyushu, mentored by [Prof. Masaaki Nagahara](https://nagahara-masaaki.github.io/).

**Research interests**

- **Applied Koopman operator theory**: dynamic mode decomposition, dynamical systems, weather dynamics
- **Decision making under uncertainty**: data-driven robust optimization, stochastic programming, randomized algorithms
- **Sparse modeling**: sparse optimization, signal processing
- **Control theory**: robust, optimal, and networked control

I am a member of SIAM, INFORMS, and IEEE.
