Hi! I am Zhicheng (Charlie) Zhang, a Specially Appointed Researcher at The University of Osaka, working with Prof. Sho Shirasaka in the Nonlinear Mathematical Science Lab.

From 2024 to 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the JST Moonshot Goal 8 Program at Kyoto University, mentored by Prof. Yoshihiko Susuki. I received my Ph.D. in Informatics from Osaka University in 2024, advised by Prof. Yasumasa Fujisaki; my M.Sc. in Mathematics from Guilin University of Electronic Technology in 2020 (advisor: Prof. Zhongjun Ma); and my B.A. in Japanese from Changzhou Institute of Technology in 2017. From 2019 to 2020, I was a visiting master’s student at The University of Kitakyushu, mentored by Prof. Masaaki Nagahara.

Research interests

  • Applied Koopman operator theory: dynamic mode decomposition, dynamical systems, weather dynamics
  • Decision making under uncertainty: data-driven robust optimization, stochastic programming, randomized algorithms
  • Sparse modeling: sparse optimization, signal processing
  • Control theory: robust, optimal, and networked control

I am a member of SIAM, INFORMS, and IEEE.

The story of my name My surname, Zhang, is passed down from my (grand)father and ancestors. My given name, Zhicheng, comes from the Chinese rendering of the proverb "Where there's a will, there's a way" — my family's hope that I become an honest, kind, and persistent person. My parents and friends usually call me Charlie.
